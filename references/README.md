# References

The project report contains the full bibliography. Third-party papers are not
redistributed. Day 4 optionally reports whether a local `lee_2024.pdf` exists
here; it does not need the PDF to run its numerical reproduction.

Key methodological sources:

- Lee, Ha, Kong and Lee (2024). *Valuing three-asset barrier options and
  autocallable products via exit probabilities of Brownian Bridge.* The North
  American Journal of Economics and Finance, 73, 102174.
- Glasserman and Staum (2001). *Conditioning on one-step survival for barrier
  option simulations.* Operations Research, 49(6), 923-937.
- Owen (1997). *Monte Carlo variance of scrambled net quadrature.* SIAM Journal
  on Numerical Analysis, 34(5), 1884-1910.
- Rakhmonov and Rakhmonov (2019). *Conditional Monte Carlo scheme for stable
  Greeks of worst-of autocallable notes.* International Journal of Theoretical
  and Applied Finance, 22(6), 1950028.
